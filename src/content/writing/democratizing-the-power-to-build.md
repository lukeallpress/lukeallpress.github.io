Vibe coding is one facet of the agentic future we're building with AI. We're not just speeding up the work of developers and tech professionals — we're democratizing the capacity to build, giving superpowers to folks who never thought they'd touch any programming language. And now they don't have to.

It was genuinely cool to run my first session on vibe coding for AzTEA (ISTE / CoSN Arizona). Coding is just one facet of how AI can be a useful assistant — but it's a powerful one. Teachers and school staff can build dashboards, custom learning experiences, and automated workflows that in the past would have required subscriptions and *still* not been exactly what they wanted to see.

And with great power comes great responsibility.

The speed and reach of these tools makes them alluring to all of us — which makes it even more important to be cautious. Don't publish tools you don't understand without having other humans review them for security and PII concerns, no matter how cool they are.

Innovate, but innovate safely.
