I got to learn with a few dozen brilliant leaders at the AzTEA CIO/CTO meetup — topics ranging from student data privacy to cyber readiness to the CoSN Trusted Learning Environment.

I also shared what our district is doing in the realm of agentic AI building, and heard other great ideas from people building out their own projects. I talked about vibe coding new features into our web apps, building an MCP server to set the stage for safe agentic tasks in the future, and our work hosting our own open-source LLMs. I learned how others are using chatbots to support AI infrastructure and interoperability, and building their own souped-up workstations for local model hosting. (I think the whole room started drooling.)

Learning is the best, and I want to keep it up. So here's my question to you:

What are you building? I want to find ways for us all to share.
