We kicked off the AzTEA Trusted Learning Environment (TLE) cohort — a commitment to learn together across Arizona to improve our data privacy practices and earn the TLE seals from the Consortium for School Networking (CoSN).

Starting this at the Curriculum/Instruction & IT Symposium felt exactly right, because taking care of student data is something we all own collectively — not just the cyber folks or the edtech team.

That's the part I care most about. Data privacy can feel like a compliance checklist handed down to a few specialists. But the daily decisions that actually protect students are made everywhere: in classrooms, in front offices, in the apps we approve and the ones we quietly let slide. A cohort makes it a shared practice instead of a siloed one.

The plan is simple: meet regularly, share our wins and our obstacles, and help each other improve. Once a month to start, then slowing down as habits take hold.

Want to learn with us? There's always room.
