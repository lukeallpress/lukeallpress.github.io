There's a lot you can do with AI without sending data to a third party — if you're willing to host the models yourself.

This work explores running open-source LLMs on district-controlled hardware: a setup that keeps sensitive data inside our walls, removes per-seat subscription costs from the equation, and gives us a sandbox to experiment in without privacy trade-offs. It pairs naturally with the [District MCP Server](/projects/district-mcp-server) — local models doing useful work behind a safe, auditable boundary.

It's also just a great way to learn how these systems actually work, instead of treating them as magic behind someone else's API.
