Open enrollment generates a steady stream of applications that each need to reach the right site's registrars and support staff. Done by hand, it's tedious and easy to get wrong — especially over seasonal breaks when the people who'd normally untangle it are out.

This tool replaces that manual routing with a custom, spreadsheet-driven workflow. Site-specific rules live in the sheet itself, so administrative staff can adjust how applications route without anyone touching code. It eliminated the need for ongoing developer maintenance and smoothed out a communication workflow that used to stall at exactly the wrong times of year.

It's a small but representative example of how I like to build for districts: solve the real operational pain, then hand the controls to the people who own the process.
