Screen time is one of those topics where districts have lots of opinions and very little shared structure. This prototype explores what it would look like to give that conversation a backbone: a tool that pairs CoSN research on digital mindfulness with a district's own local context to help leaders plan more intentional approaches to student device use.

Right now it's a **front-end-only shell** — the interface and the framing are in place, but no AI is connected yet. It's an early sketch of the experience, built to test whether the idea is worth developing further before wiring up anything behind it.
