Partnerships are one of the most valuable things a district has — and one of the easiest to lose track of. Contacts live in inboxes, agreements live in someone's drive, and the institutional memory walks out the door when a person leaves.

The Partnership Tracker pulls all of that into a single, simple interface: active partners, the people involved, what each partnership delivers, and the status of every relationship at a glance. It replaces a tangle of spreadsheets with one source of truth that staff across the district can actually use.

It's also a small case study in building district-owned software instead of renting it — fast to build, shaped exactly to how we work, and ours to change whenever the work changes.
