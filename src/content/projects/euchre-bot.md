Not everything has to be a district initiative. Sometimes the best way to learn a tool — and to show others it's approachable — is to build something fun.

The Euchre Bot plays the card game euchre well enough to be a real opponent. It's a small, self-contained demo I use to make the abstract concrete: when I tell educators they can build things now, this is the kind of thing I point to. It's tangible, it's playable, and it makes the leap from "AI is a buzzword" to "oh, *I* could make something" feel a lot shorter.
