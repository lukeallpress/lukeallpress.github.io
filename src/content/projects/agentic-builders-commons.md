Most "AI in education" conversations stay at the awareness stage. The Agentic Builders Committee is a group of K12 technology leaders from districts across the country who are past that — building, deploying, and governing AI agents in production school systems. I built and run the commons that holds their shared work.

The site is the group's working hub, organized around the things practitioners actually need:

- **The Practitioners** — the district CTOs, directors, and superintendents doing the work
- **Ideas Worth Stealing** — reusable patterns for data access, governance, and operations
- **Projects & Recipes** — working implementations paired to the problems they solve
- **Open Questions** — the hard, unresolved challenges the group is investigating together
- **Call Archive** — notes from the monthly syncs

The whole point is to stop everyone from rebuilding the same foundations alone: collaborate on shared infrastructure, set safety and governance practices together, and pass around code patterns that already work. It's the most complete expression of how I think about this moment — the ecosystem is the strategy, not any single tool.
